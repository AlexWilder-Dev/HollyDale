The pub

Back on the corner.

The Hollydale Tavern closed in 2019. In 2024 new owners gave the building a full refurbishment and opened the doors again as The Hollydale.

It sits on a quiet residential street on the Peckham/Nunhead border, and it's very much the local.

History

Since 1878.

There's been a pub on this corner since at least 1878, when it was first listed as 45 Halton Terrace.

    First listed

    Listed as 45 Halton Terrace, with Joseph Simmons as landlord.
