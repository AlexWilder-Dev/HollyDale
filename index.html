The story

Boarded up in 2021. Pouring again in 2024.

The Hollydale has been a pub since 1878. It closed in 2019, the floors above were turned into flats, and the bar sat shuttered on the corner of Brayards Road.

Richard, a hospitality professional who has opened plenty of venues before, first walked in in 2021, while it was still boarded up. After years of hard work, planning and investment, the doors opened again in 2024 on a bright, modern, minimal room with a beautiful wooden bar.

The old Hollydale Tavern lettering was painted out. Inside and on the menus it's The Hollydale Peckham, or THP for short.

Inside

High ceilings, a wooden bar and a row of cinema seats.

One bright, open room with big windows onto both streets, built around the bar. Local artist Fran Giffard's birds hang on the walls.

Food

The famous one is the double smash.

The cheeseburger is made in-house from local produce: two smashed beef patties, Monterey Jack, pickled onions, gherkins and THP burger sauce. The rest of the menu runs from small plates to bavette steak.

The garden

Out the back, a walled garden.

Picnic benches, festoon lights and a wall-sized mural for El Rayo, the tequila with Peckham roots. The regulars rate it highly.
